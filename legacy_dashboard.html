SEC-02: Solid Waste Management

Waste Classification & Lifecycle

Municipal (MSW)

Organic and inorganic everyday refuse from residential and commercial sources.

Hazardous

Ignitable, corrosive, reactive, or toxic materials.

Biomedical

Infectious materials and sharps requiring high-temp sterilization.

Treatment & Disposal Matrix

Sanitary Landfill
Controlled burial with impermeable liners (clay/synthetic) and leachate collection systems to prevent groundwater contamination.
Incineration
Aerobic combustion providing significant volume reduction and heat recovery.
Pyrolysis
Anaerobic thermal decay producing high-value chemical feedstocks (combustible gases, bio-oil, char).

Resource Recovery

Prioritizing energy and material recovery via the 4R Framework (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle, Recover) and Industrial Treatment.

Biological Processing

  • Composting: Aerobic decomposition to soil amendment.
  • Anaerobic Digestion: O2-free breakdown producing methane biogas.

Industrial Effluent

Removing toxic heavy metals and pollutants from wastewater (ETP) and municipal sewage (STP) before discharge.

Refuse Derived Fuel (RDF)

High-calorific components sorted via Mechanical Biological Treatment (MBT) to substitute coal in power plants.
